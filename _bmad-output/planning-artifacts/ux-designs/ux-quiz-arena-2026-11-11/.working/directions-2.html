Minnal: Quiet Focus, round 2

Four variations on the round-1 pick. All keep the same base: lots of space, large Malayalam with tall line spacing, one hairline timer, scores kept small, and almost nothing to render. They differ in how loud the peak moments get, in warmth, and in light vs dark. Each row shows three screens: the live Q4 with the answer locked, the reveal, and the Battle Room scoreboard just after you overtake someone. There's no JS, so the motion notes under each screen say what would move.

A Pure Quiet refined round-1, calm all the way through

This is round-1 Quiet Focus with the rough edges fixed. The teal becomes a slate-blue ink, the paper gets warmer, and the hierarchy is cleaner: the question gets the screen and everything else steps back. Peaks are not dramatised at all. Rank changes and +800 come across through position and number alone, like a well-made study app that trusts the player.
