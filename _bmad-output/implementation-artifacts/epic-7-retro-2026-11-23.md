## Acceptance verdict

**Accepted-with-open-items** (criteria declared). Every story is `done` and `pending_stories` is empty. The epic goal's deliverables exist: a share card with a `/q` link, a once-only install card with CI installability checks, OTLP metrics with an allowlist, the seven launch metrics, dashboards and alerts, a restore runbook with a proven drill and a decide-versus-build checklist. The open items are real but tracked: the independent review was never run (F7), the test-stability problems (F8, F9), and everything that needs a phone, the VPS or the owner's accounts (F11, the launch checklist). The epic does **not** make Minnal launch-ready: every owner item is open and blocks launch, and Story 1.2 part 2 is not built. That is a statement about launch, not a failed Epic 7 criterion.
