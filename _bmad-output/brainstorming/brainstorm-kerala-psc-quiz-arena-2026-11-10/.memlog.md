- (decision) MVP game modes locked: Quick Match (1v1 + bot fallback), Topic Duel (1v1 topic-locked), Battle Room (2-N players free-for-all live scoreboard). Extend later.

- (decision) App name decided: Minnal (മിന്നൽ — lightning)
